Mission

TUD is The Ugly Duck who doesn't need wings to fly. He reaches the top with his confidence, courage and bold.

He is forever young rebel and seeker influenced by freedom and independence: no boundaries, no stereotypes. Understanding his strengths and embracing his weaknesses TUD lives by his achievements.

We have created a unique image that conveys our aspirations and worldview. A character for whom being different is the strongest trait.

TUD toy is the physical attribute of a new layer of society. He is confident and this is noticed not only in his character but also in his appearance. It's not about who he was, it's about who he has become.

I'm not afraid to make mistakes. It turned out to be a great experience because it allowed me to grow.

I'm not afraid to go where no one has ever been before. After all, it motivates me for new achievements. I steadfastly and patiently endure difficulties and hardships. This is how I strengthen the spirit.

TUD's history and values are built around selfmade individuals, new technologies and art. The project was created to bring together people who are similar in spirit. Innovators who are used to surrounding themselves with like-minded people.

The Ugly Duck cultivates limitless possibilities and the power of human, regardless of what was originally.
